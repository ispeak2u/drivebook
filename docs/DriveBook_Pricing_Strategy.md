# DriveBook Pricing Strategy


DriveBook Pricing Strategy

Objective
Create a pricing model that makes it easy for Toronto driving instructors to join DriveBook while generating sustainable revenue.

Phase 1: Launch Strategy

Instructor Pricing
- Free to join
- No setup fee
- No monthly fee
- Fixed $60 lesson price, set by the platform (not the instructor)
- Instructor receives $45 per completed lesson; DriveBook retains $15 (25% platform take rate)

Student Pricing
- Free to use DriveBook
- Students pay the fixed $60 lesson fee

Why This Works
- Removes risk for instructors
- Easier instructor acquisition
- Revenue starts immediately from successful bookings

Example
Lesson Fee: CAD $60
DriveBook Retains: CAD $15 (25%)
Instructor Receives: CAD $45

Phase 2: DriveBook Pro

After:
- 25+ active instructors
- 500+ completed bookings

Phase 2 pricing (Pro plans, tiered commission) is out of scope until Phase 1 is proven.

Competitive Advantages
- Verified reviews
- Online booking
- Automated scheduling
- Cancellation accountability
- Language matching
- Pickup pin locations
- Reminder notifications

Launch Goals
- 25 instructors onboarded
- 100 students registered
- 100 completed bookings

Long-Term Revenue Opportunities
- Instructor subscriptions
- Featured listings
- Road test packages
- Advertising partnerships
- Premium verification

Recommendation
Launch with free instructor signup and the fixed $60 lesson price (instructor receives $45, DriveBook retains $15, 25% platform take rate).
Phase 2 pricing is out of scope until Phase 1 is proven.
